I — The table

Republic Georgian Restaurant is on the third floor of the Republic building on First Republic Square. Its menu was curated by Brand Chef Irakli Asatiani: the traditions of Georgian cuisine alongside dishes created exclusively for Republic.

Inside, a dining room with the city behind the glass. Outside, an open-air terrace with panoramic views of the Tbilisi skyline — for relaxed evenings, lively gatherings and celebrations.

    The square

    A square with a past

    Republic Square in the 1980s. Archive photographs: Republic

    1. 1983

      Republic Square is completed with architect O. Kalandarishvili’s concrete arches — the only finished part of a larger project. They become a parade stand, nicknamed “Andropov’s Ears”.

    2. 2005

      The structure is dismantled and the square is renamed Rose Revolution Square.

    3. 2018

      On 25 May, for the centenary of the Democratic Republic of Georgia, it becomes First Republic Square.
