I — The table

Republic is a Georgian restaurant on First Republic Square in Tbilisi. Its menu of modern Georgian flavours and signature dishes was created by Brand Chef Irakli Asatiani.

Come for dinner and stay for the evening: from the open-air terrace above the square, the city opens up in a panorama.
